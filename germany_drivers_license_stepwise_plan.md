# Step-by-Step Guide: Obtaining a Driver's License in Germany (Class B)

This guide outlines the complete, structured process for obtaining a standard Class B passenger car driver's license (*Führerschein*) in Germany.

---

## 1. Prerequisites & Documentation

Before enrolling or applying to the local driving authority, assemble the following mandatory documents and certificates:

* **First Aid Certificate (*Erste Hilfe Kurs*):**
  * Mandatory 9-unit (approx. 7.5 hours) training course covering basic life-saving measures.
* **Eye Examination (*Sehtest*):**
  * Official vision test conducted by an optometrist, ophthalmologist, or certified driving school (valid for 2 years).
* **Biometric Passport Photo:**
  * One recent standard biometric photo adhering to German official requirements.
* **Identification & Residency:**
  * Valid Passport or National Identity Card.
  * Certificate of Residence Registration (*Meldebescheinigung* or *Anmeldebestätigung*).

---

## 2. Fahrschule (Driving School) Registration

Choose and contract with a state-certified driving school (*Fahrschule*):

* **Language Support:** Verify if theory lectures, study apps, and instructional materials are available in English.
* **License Transmission Category:**
  * **Class B (Manual):** Standard training on a manual gearbox (allows driving both manual and automatic).
  * **Class B78 (Automatic only):** Training and examination on automatic; restricts driving to automatic vehicles only.
  * **Class B197 (Hybrid Option):** Take the practical driving test in an automatic vehicle while completing a minimum of 10 driving lessons plus a 15-minute evaluation drive in a manual car. Grants an unrestricted license to drive both manual and automatic.

---

## 3. Application at the Licensing Authority (*Führerscheinstelle*)

Submit the official application (*Fahrerlaubnisantrag*) to your local city office (*Bürgeramt* / *Führerscheinstelle*):

1. **Submission:** Driving schools frequently handle submission, or you may book an appointment directly.
2. **Background Check & Verification:** Authorities check background records (*Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt* / central register).
3. **Approval (*Prüfauftrag*):** The office issues an official examination mandate to an accredited testing organization (TÜV or DEKRA).
4. **Processing Time:** Usually takes **4 to 8 weeks**.

---

## 4. Theory Training (*Theorieunterricht*)

Attend the legally prescribed classroom hours:

* **Basic Material (*Grundstoff*):** 12 double sessions (90 minutes each).
* **Class B Add-on (*Zusatzstoff*):** 2 double sessions (90 minutes each).
* **Self-Study:** Prepare using official digital learning software/apps covering the official question bank (available in 12 languages).

---

## 5. Theoretical Examination (*Theorieprüfung*)

Once the testing mandate (*Prüfauftrag*) is active and mandatory classroom sessions are logged:

* **Format:** Computerized multiple-choice test at a TÜV/DEKRA test center.
* **Structure:** 30 questions randomly drawn from a pool of >1,000 questions.
* **Passing Threshold:** Maximum of 10 error points allowed (except failing automatically if answering two 5-point questions incorrectly).
* **Validity:** Valid for **12 months** from the date of passing to complete the practical exam.

---

## 6. Practical Driving Instruction (*Fahrstunden*)

Behind-the-wheel instruction consists of two phases:

### A. Basic Training (*Grundausbildung*)
* Customized number of lessons depending on individual skill, focusing on vehicle mechanics, urban traffic navigation, lane discipline, right-of-way (*Rechts vor Links*), and standard parking maneuvers.

### B. Mandatory Special Journeys (*Sonderfahrten*)
Total of **12 mandatory 45-minute sessions**:
* **5 sessions:** Country roads and highways (*Überlandfahrt*).
* **4 sessions:** Autobahn driving (*Autobahnfahrt*).
* **3 sessions:** Night/twilight driving (*Beleuchtungs-/Dunkelfahrt*).

---

## 7. Practical Driving Examination (*Praktische Prüfung*)

The concluding driving assessment with an official TÜV/DEKRA examiner:

* **Duration:** Approximately 45 to 55 minutes.
* **Technical Inspection Questions:** Safety check of tires, engine fluid levels, lights, or dashboard warning indicators.
* **Driving Assessment:**
  * Complex urban traffic, roundabouts, speed limit adherence (*Zone 30*, *Spielstraße*).
  * Autobahn / high-speed rural stretches.
  * Three technical driving tasks (*Grundfahraufgaben*): e.g., parallel parking, turning around, emergency stop (*Gefahrenbremsung*).
* **Outcome:** The examiner announces the result immediately after the drive and provides either the permanent driving license or a temporary test certificate (*Prüfungsbescheinigung*).

---

## 8. Post-Acquisition Notes

* **Probationary Period (*Probezeit*):** A mandatory 2-year trial period begins immediately upon receipt. Traffic violations classified as category 'A' (e.g., speeding >20 km/h) trigger a 2-year extension (total 4 years) and an advanced seminar (*Aufbauseminar*).
* **Blood Alcohol Limit:** Strict **0.00‰** limit applies throughout the entire 2-year *Probezeit* and for all drivers under the age of 21.
